## 2. Pain — "the SMB CEO doesn't know what to do with AI"

| # | Cluster | Core pain | Current workarounds |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | Strategic anxiety | "Is AI an existential threat to my business model — or an opportunity I'm missing?" No structured way to answer | Press, peers, ad-hoc event conversations |
| P2 | Process blindness | Can't tell which of THEIR OWN processes would benefit from AI; generic advice doesn't map to their operations | Copying a peer; letting one employee experiment; doing nothing |
| P3 | Prioritization paralysis | Vendors push tools, consultants push missions; no neutral value-vs-feasibility ranking → paralysis or scattered pilots | Vendor demos, free "AI maturity" quizzes, gut feel |
| P4 | Execution gap | Even with intuition, no in-house skill to turn it into a roadmap teams can act on | Costly consultant, delegating to IT, deferring |

Pain intensity: high, recurrent in every workshop cohort [FOUNDER-ASSERTED]. Buying intent: asserted, unverified by purchase evidence.

## 3. Opportunity & idea

**One-sentence idea:** for SMB CEOs, a guided AI diagnostic that scores (1) **Strategy** — whether AI threatens or boosts their business model — and (2) **Operations** — which of their processes AI can realistically improve (value × feasibility) — producing a prioritized, plain-language action plan.

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Main user outcome | The CEO knows, with justified priorities, what to do — and not do — about AI in the next 12/18 months |
| V1 core capabilities (macro) | Strategy diagnostic (threat/opportunity on business model) · process-level AI-eligibility scoring (value × feasibility) · auto-prioritization (quick wins / strategic / defer) · generated plain-language report |
| Current alternatives | Free big-4 "AI maturity" lead magnets, generic quizzes, vendor demos, peer imitation, inaction [INFERENCE — no competitor sweep] |
| Differentiation | SMB **specific/custom analysis and recommendations** (no generic slop) · process-level scoring (vs corporate maturity models) · neutral — not selling implementation |
| Monetization | Paid diagnostic / consultant-assisted missions; pricing model to be clarified (reference point: ai4business prototype sold €199/€249 modules) |
